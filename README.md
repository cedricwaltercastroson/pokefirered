# Pokémon FireRed

This is a disassembly of Pokémon FireRed.

To set up the repository, see [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/cedricwaltercastroson/pokefirered/blob/main/INSTALL.md).

## Features

- Sevii Islands removed to keep the classic Kanto experience.
- All 151 original Pokémon are obtainable in a single ROM.
- Moltres can be found at Victory Road, as in the original games.
- A level 5 roaming Mew becomes available after defeating the Pokémon League, with its obedience flag set.
- The Move Deleter, Move Tutor, and Starter Ultimate Move Tutor share a house in Fuchsia City and offer their services for free.
- Trading and battling on the second floor of Pokémon Centers are disabled.
- Pokémon automatically recover move PP after battles.
- Pokémon caught in the Safari Zone are placed in standard Poké Balls.
- EXP Share is enabled for the party without an item.
- Saving requires fewer confirmation prompts.
- All Pokémon are genderless, as in Generation 1.
- Pokémon can follow the player throughout Kanto, with species-specific sprites and shiny colours.
- Players can interact with their following Pokémon.
- Following Pokémon accompany the player through the overworld, including field moves and Pokémon League events.
- Following Pokémon automatically return after wild battles and Gym Leader rematches.
- A following lead Pokémon slides into battle without being released from a Poké Ball.
- All eight Kanto Gym Leaders can be challenged again after becoming Champion, with no PokéNav or cooldown required.
- Gym Leader rematches feature four progressively stronger, anime-inspired teams, with the final team repeatable.
- Giovanni returns to Viridian Gym after the Pokémon League.
- A Pokémon Crystal-inspired day and night cycle alternates every 30 minutes.
- Nighttime colours extend to the overworld, wild battles, Trainer Battles, and Safari Zone.
- Players can sleep in their bedroom in Pallet Town.
- Dynamic weather includes rain and thunderstorms.
